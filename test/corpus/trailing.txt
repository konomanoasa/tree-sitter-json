==================
Trailing commas are normal source in nonempty containers
:language(jsonc_trailing)
==================
{"a":[1,/*x*/],}
---
(json_text
  (value
    (object
      (member
        name: (string (char (unescaped)))
        value: (value
          (array
            (value (number (int (digit1_9))))
            (multi_line_comment (comment_text))))))))

==================
EOF after an object comma requires only its closing
:language(jsonc_trailing)
==================
{"a":1,
---
(json_text
  (value
    (object
      (member
        name: (string (char (unescaped)))
        value: (value (number (int (digit1_9)))))
      issue: (syntax_issue (incomplete_syntax (missing_object_close))))))

==================
A second trailing comma remains an actual separator issue
:language(jsonc_trailing)
==================
[1,,]
---
(json_text
  (value
    (array
      (value (number (int (digit1_9))))
      (syntax_issue (invalid_syntax (unexpected_separator))))))
